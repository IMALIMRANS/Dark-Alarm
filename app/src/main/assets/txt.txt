1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

---

Dark Alarm v2.0 is a feature-rich Android alarm clock application designed with modern Jetpack Compose UI, Room database, alarm scheduling, custom sound player, and a local computer vision image matching challenge ("Scan Object").

The current APK published on APKPure is fully functional. However, in its present state on the main branch, the codebase CANNOT be accepted into the official F-Droid repository because it links against proprietary Google/Firebase SDKs (Firebase AI and Firebase App Check reCAPTCHA) and applies the Google Services Gradle plugin.

The core local features (Room DB, AlarmScheduler, custom audio playback, and local dHash/aHash/HOG image matching in ScanMatcherEngine) are 100% FOSS-compatible and execute entirely on-device without cloud or proprietary dependencies.

To prepare the app for F-Droid without breaking the existing APKPure release, a dual-target Gradle Product Flavors approach ("full" vs "fdroid") is required.

Overall Assessment: 🟡 REQUIRES CHANGES (F-Droid Build Variant & License File needed).

2. 🟢 F-DROID COMPATIBLE

---

- Local Computer Vision Engine (ScanMatcherEngine.kt): Implements dHash, aHash, HOG, and color histogram analysis 100% locally on Android CPU via standard Kotlin bitmap algorithms without ML Kit or external APIs.
- Room Local Database (AppDatabase.kt, AlarmDao.kt, AlarmRepository.kt): Clean local SQLite persistence using standard Android Jetpack Room with KSP.
- Alarm Scheduling Infrastructure (AlarmScheduler.kt, AlarmReceiver.kt, AlarmRingingService.kt, BootReceiver.kt): Uses Android AlarmManager, WakeLock, and Foreground Services properly.
- UI Framework: 100% Jetpack Compose with Material 3 design system.
- Jetpack & AndroidX Core Libraries: Core-ktx, Lifecycle, Activity Compose, Navigation Compose, CameraX, WorkManager.
- Third-Party FOSS Libraries: Coil Compose (Apache 2.0), Kotlinx Serialization (Apache 2.0).
- Permissions: All permissions requested in AndroidManifest.xml are legitimate and necessary for a reliable alarm app (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, CAMERA, WAKE_LOCK, SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW).

6. FIREBASE / GEMINI AUDIT

---

- What is used:
  1. `com.google.firebase:firebase-ai:16.2.0` is used for remote Gemini model interactions (e.g., generating math challenges or processing prompt requests if configured).
  2. `com.google.firebase:firebase-appcheck-recaptcha:16.1.2` is used for App Check attestation protecting the Firebase backend.
  3. `com.google.gms.google-services` plugin handles Google services initialization.

- F-Droid Compatibility Status:
  The Firebase AI and App Check SDKs are proprietary Google client libraries. Official F-Droid repository policies strictly forbid bundling proprietary libraries.
  However, the app's local fallback features (local MathChallengeEngine and local ScanMatcherEngine) function completely offline without Firebase or Gemini.

- Recommendation for F-Droid:
  Use Gradle Product Flavors (`full` vs `fdroid`). In the `fdroid` variant, disable Firebase/Gemini SDKs entirely or replace them with a generic, direct HTTP/REST open-source client calling an optional user-provided custom API key endpoint.

8. PERMISSION AUDIT

---

- android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Required to schedule precise alarm wake-up triggers via AlarmManager.
- android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM: Required on Android 13+ to ensure exact alarm timing.
- android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Required to reschedule alarms after device reboot (BootReceiver.kt).
- android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Required to keep the alarm ringing service active.
- android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required on Android 14+ to play alarm audio streams in foreground service.
- android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to display ringing notifications and alarm status.
- android.permission.CAMERA: Required for reference photo capture and live preview scanning in ScanChallengeView.kt.
- android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Required to awaken CPU and turn on screen when an alarm fires.
- android.permission.DISABLE_KEYGUARD: Required to show AlarmRingingActivity over the device lock screen.
- android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Required for emergency full-screen alarm overlay presentation.
- android.permission.VIBRATE: Required to trigger haptic vibration during alarm ringing.
- android.permission.REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Required to prevent OEM Android battery savers from killing scheduled alarms.
